# Ten General Regulatory Requirements Gaps

**Reviewed:** September 30, 2026. This public-documentation review consolidates all 57 generic entries in `requirements.txt` into control-assurance gaps to evaluate for a hypothetical protected health information (PHI) environment. The rows identify evidence an assessment must obtain, not verified tenant defects or a compliance certification. No customer-specific configuration or approval decisions are represented.

**Baseline:** Applicable HIPAA/HITECH safeguards, not mandatory use of particular Fabric features. Operational constraints are design risks unless linked to an applicable obligation. Regulatory text was reviewed using the September 28, 2026 eCFR version.[^R1]

Distinguish tenant-level from workspace-level Private Link and establish compatible SQL, external-engine, gateway, AI, sharing, and reporting paths. Endpoint availability alone does not prove a working private integration. | Entries 2, 4, 5, 6, 9, 39, 41, 42; 45 CFR 164.312(a), (e).[^R1][^M1][^M2][^M14][^M20] |

SQL auditing is opt-in and best-effort, and cannot alone cover every access path. Establish suitable change records and snapshots rather than relying only on current views. Domains organize data but do not enforce item authorization. Tenant-managed policies can have narrower targets; a proposed dependency must not be credited as implemented until its approval and behavior are evidenced. | Entries 24, 25, 36, 37, 38, 57; 45 CFR 164.308(a)(2), (a)(4).[^R1][^M10][^M11] |
| **G07 - Reliable monitoring and operational availability** | Monitoring availability, telemetry selection, sharing permissions, capacity funding, and alert resilience have not been validated for the proposed configuration. Standard deployment pipelines do not support restricted workspaces, so an approved alternative is needed. Inventory the full estate, permissions, and capacity implications; distinguish per-study/environment multipliers from shared and recovery overhead without double counting. | Entries 8, 55; 45 CFR 164.308(a)(1), (a)(8).[^R1][^M1] |

Distinguish workspace CMK from Power BI BYOK and verify protection per artifact. Where data is intended to be de-identified, validate Safe Harbor or Expert Determination; masking or encryption alone does not establish that status. | Entries 32, 47, 49, 53; 45 CFR 164.312(a)(2)(iv), 164.514(a)-(b).[^R1][^R3][^M3][^M12][^M13] |

Establish applicable business associate agreement (BAA) coverage, versioned architecture evidence, control mapping, and approved compensating measures. ## Evidence qualifications

- **Networking:** Entry 5's blanket absence of a private SQL FQDN is contradicted by current documentation; the Databricks integration itself was not tested. Entry 39 is also overbroad: Capacity Metrics supports tenant-level Private Link, but not workspace-level Private Link where the app is installed.[^M1][^M14]
- **Monitoring:** Updated documentation describes Private Link support and configurable retention with a 30-day default. Several source limits describe legacy monitoring. However, the workspace Private Link support matrix still lists monitoring as unsupported; this documentation conflict needs configuration-specific confirmation, not a categorical verdict.[^M1][^M8]
- **Identity, history, and encryption:** Permission-change auditing exists; historical effective-access reconstruction is a separate assurance. Storage-level owner attribution is not proof that all end-user evidence is lost. Workspace CMK and capacity BYOK have different artifact coverage, including documented preview coexistence; a reporting-workspace CMK designation requires artifact-level evidence.[^M3][^M5][^M6][^M12][^M21]
- **Lifecycle and eligibility:** Entry 1's cutoff is **October 1, 2026**, and managed-solution support ends **December 31, 2027**. Preview protections and exceptions require feature-specific BAA review. Microsoft publishes a healthcare/patient-data security scenario, while Azure TRE is a best-effort accelerator; neither establishes operating compliance for a proposed architecture. Blanket reference-absence claims require verification, and no independent NIST assessment is claimed.[^M15][^M16][^M17]
